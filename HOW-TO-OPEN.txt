ResumeLayer — landing page preview for review (static build)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip the archive (keep the folder as it is).
2. Double-click "index.html" (or drag it into Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
No installation, Node.js or server is needed. Internet is not required.

WHAT TO KNOW
- This is a design/copy preview, not the product. Nothing is uploaded or processed.
- The "Join the waitlist" form is a demo: it only checks the email format and
  shows that sign-up is not available yet. The email is not saved or sent anywhere.
- The resume cards use fictional sample data ("Alex Morgan"). The "94% ATS Score"
  badge is a static illustration, not a real feature.
- Keep the "_next" folder next to index.html; the page needs it.

КАК ОТКРЫТЬ (RU)
1. Распакуйте архив (не меняя структуру папки).
2. Дважды кликните по файлу index.html — страница откроется в браузере.
Установка, Node.js и сервер не нужны, интернет не требуется.
Форма Waitlist — демо: email не сохраняется и никуда не отправляется.
